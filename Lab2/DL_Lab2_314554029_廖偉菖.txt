# Data Preprocessing & Augmentation
Trimaps were mapped to binary masks. To aggressively prevent overfitting over 500 epochs, albumentations was used for Resize(192), horizontal flips, affine transforms, and brightness adjustments. Crucially, to simulate biological shape variations as mentioned in U-Net, I applied ElasticTransform (alpha=120, sigma=12), producing organic warps. ImageNet standardization was implemented directly inside __getitem__() to guarantee train/val/test consistency. Masks were explicitly expanded to (1, H, W) to perfectly align with logits.

# Model Implementation
Models were built independently from scratch. The baseline U-Net strictly adheres to the 2015 paper, excluding BatchNorm and using unpadded convolutions. I implemented the "overlap-tile strategy" via reflection padding, allowing exact 192x192 crop-and-concatenate alignment. Conversely, ResNet34_UNet utilizes padded convolutions, BatchNorm, and custom "Up" blocks for asymmetrical residual concatenation. To maximize accuracy during inference, Test-Time Augmentation (TTA) was applied by averaging probabilities from the original and horizontally flipped images. Finally, predictions were dynamically resized back to original dimensions via nearest-neighbor interpolation.

# Training Strategy
Models were trained using BCEWithLogitsLoss and Adam Optimizer for 500 epochs. A CosineAnnealingLR scheduler was integrated to enable fast initial learning followed by smooth, fine-grained convergence.

# Observations
ResNet34_UNet significantly outperformed the baseline. Its deep residual encoder successfully mitigated vanishing gradients and extracted robust semantic features, yielding sharper pet boundaries and superior generalization on the unseen test set.
