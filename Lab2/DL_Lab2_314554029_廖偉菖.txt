# Data Preprocessing & Augmentation
Trimaps were mapped to binary masks (pet=1, background=0).
To prevent overfitting, albumentations was used for Resize(256, 256), horizontal flips, affine transforms, and brightness adjustments.
Crucially, to ensure strict distribution consistency across train/val/test phases, ImageNet standardization was implemented directly inside the __getitem__ method. Mask dimensions were also explicitly expanded to (1, H, W) to perfectly align with logits, preventing PyTorch broadcasting errors during loss calculation.

# Model Implementation
Both UNet and ResNet34_UNet were built from scratch.
A key highlight was designing a dynamic "Up" block to handle asymmetrical channel concatenations from the ResNet34 skip connections.
During inference, predicted 256x256 masks were resized back to original dimensions via nearest-neighbor interpolation, preserving sharp boundaries for RLE encoding.

# Training Strategy
Models were optimized using BCEWithLogitsLoss and Adam Optimizer over 500 epochs.
I integrated a CosineAnnealingLR scheduler. This scheduling strategy allowed the model to learn rapidly in the early stages and smoothly decay the learning rate later, enabling fine-grained convergence into the optimal local minimum.

# Observations
ResNet34_UNet significantly outperformed the baseline UNet.
Its deep residual encoder effectively mitigated vanishing gradients, extracting richer and more robust semantic features early on. This empowered the decoder to reconstruct highly accurate pet boundaries and achieve superior generalization over the 500 epochs.
