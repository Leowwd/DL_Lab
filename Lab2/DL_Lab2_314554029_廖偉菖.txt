# Data Preprocessing & Augmentation
Trimap annotations were converted to binary masks by mapping label 1 (pet) to foreground (1), and labels 2/3 to background (0).
To prevent overfitting during the extended 500-epoch training, on-the-fly data augmentation was implemented using the Albumentations library.
Techniques included random horizontal flips, affine transformations (scaling, translation, rotation with constant value padding), and brightness/contrast adjustments. For masks, nearest-neighbor interpolation was strictly applied.

# Implementation Highlights
Both UNet and ResNet34_UNet were implemented from scratch without pretrained weights.
A major highlight was designing a robust "Up" block in the decoder to handle asymmetrical channel concatenation, ensuring the ResNet34 encoder's skip connections merged flawlessly.
During inference, predicted 256x256 masks were dynamically resized back to their original dimensions using nearest-neighbor interpolation before RLE encoding to meet Kaggle's exact evaluation criteria.

# Training Strategy
Models were trained using BCEWithLogitsLoss and the Adam optimizer (lr=1e-4) with a batch size of 16 for 500 epochs.
Validation Dice Score was calculated after each epoch, and the model state yielding the highest validation score was saved.

# Observations: UNet vs ResNet34_UNet
While the baseline UNet successfully captured spatial details, the ResNet34_UNet demonstrated superior performance and significantly faster convergence.
The ResNet34 encoder's deep residual architecture effectively mitigated vanishing gradients, extracting richer and more robust semantic features early on.
This enabled the decoder to reconstruct sharper pet boundaries, maximizing generalization and accuracy over the 500 epochs.
